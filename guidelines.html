WELCOME TO IPL BOOKING

Book your tickets for the hottest cricket matches

  • Carry a valid ticket to enter the stadium.
  • Carry a valid ID proof.
  • Reach the stadium at least 1 hour before the match.
  • Do not carry prohibited items inside the stadium.
  • Follow the security instructions at the stadium.
  • Keep your ticket safe until the end of the match.
  • Do not damage or litter the stadium premises.
  • Follow the seating instructions mentioned on your ticket.
  • Tickets once booked cannot be cancelled or transferred.
  • Maintain proper discipline and enjoy the match responsibly.
